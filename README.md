## 🌐 Overview

Neon Cue Club is a full 8-ball pool game set in a neon-lit pool hall. You can play against three computer opponents, take turns with a friend on the same device, practise on a free table, or try eight trick shots. It runs in any modern browser on desktop and phones, with no install or build step.

## ✨ Key Features

✅ **Three computer opponents** - Progress from rookie to shark, and win to climb the club ladder.

✅ **Two-player mode** - Take turns on the same device.

✅ **Practice table** - A free table with undo and ball-in-hand.

✅ **Trick shots** - Eight challenges that earn stars. Fewer tries means more stars.

✅ **Spin control** - Backspin, follow and english on the cue ball.

✅ **Aim guide** - Long, short or off.

✅ **Mobile support** - Works on phones as well as desktop.

## 🚀 How to Play

Clone or download this repository, then open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Input |
| --- | --- |
| Turn the cue | Drag on the table, or `←` `→` / `A` `D` (hold `Shift` for fine steps) |
| Aim straight at a ball | Click the ball |
| Shoot | Drag the power bar down and release, or hold `Space` to charge and release |
| Spin | Drag the red dot on the cue ball. Low for backspin, high for follow, sides for english |
| Switch cue view / top-down view | `V` |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel / `W` `S` |
| Aim guide (long, short, off) | `G` |
| Pause | `Esc` |

## 📏 8-Ball Rules

- The table is **open** after the break. Pot a ball to claim **Solids (1–7)** or **Stripes (9–15)**.
- Pot one of your balls and you shoot again. If you miss, your opponent's turn begins.
- **Fouls** give your opponent ball in hand. A foul happens when you pot the cue ball, hit nothing, or hit a ball from the wrong group first.
- Clear your group, then pot the **8-ball** to win. Potting it early or scratching on it loses the game.
